Awards & Recognition

Presentations are evaluated independently by expert panels to recognize top-tier academic contributions across all disciplines.

Sector-Wise Awards

Dedicated Best Oral and Best Poster awards presented distinctly for each of the 5 academic tracks.

Grand Symposium Prize

One overall prestigious award given for the single most outstanding research presentation across all tracks.

Outstanding Fellow

Special recognition awarded by mentors and the NSSR Research Team for fellowship excellence.
