Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about tracks, abstracts, and presentations.

Who can submit an abstract to the symposium?

Abstract submission is open to NSRF Fellows, university students, faculty members, and independent researchers. Submissions are accepted for both oral and poster presentations across all five tracks.

Can I submit my abstract to more than one track?

No. Each abstract may be submitted to one track only. If you are unsure which track fits your research best, contact us at nsrf@nssrnepal.org and we will help you decide before the deadline.

Is a full research paper required at submission time?

No. Only an abstract is required for submission. Selected high-impact research may afterwards be recommended for peer-review and publication in partnership with the Nepal Physical Society (NPS).

What is the presentation format?

Oral presentations run for 12 minutes followed by a 3-minute Q&A in parallel breakout sessions. Poster presentations are evaluated interactively during the designated poster session. Both formats are eligible for sector-wise awards.

Can I present more than one abstract?

Yes, but each presenter may present at most one oral and one poster presentation. Each presentation requires a separate abstract submission and registration.

When is the abstract submission deadline?

The early-bird deadline is November 1, 2026, and the regular deadline is November 20, 2026. Submit through the registration page well before the deadline to secure your slot.

Who can I contact for further questions?

Email us at nsrf@nssrnepal.org or call +977 9826716653 / 9843697197 for any queries regarding tracks, abstracts, or presentations.

Awards & Recognition

Presentations are evaluated independently by expert panels to recognize top-tier academic contributions across all disciplines.

Sector-Wise Awards

Dedicated Best Oral and Best Poster awards presented distinctly for each of the 5 academic tracks.

Grand Symposium Prize

One overall prestigious award given for the single most outstanding research presentation across all tracks.

Outstanding Fellow

Special recognition awarded by mentors and the NSSR Research Team for fellowship excellence.
