Research Dissemination

Symposium Publications & Journals

A core objective of the NSSR is to ensure student research reaches the wider scientific community. We partner with established academic bodies to offer publication pathways for outstanding abstracts presented at the symposium.

Nepal Physical Society (NPS)

Official Publication Partner

Publishing with NPS

We are proud to collaborate with the Nepal Physical Society (NPS). Selected high-impact research papers from the Physical Sciences, Materials Science, and Computational tracks will be recommended for peer-review and subsequent publication in the NPS-affiliated journals and symposium proceedings.

  • Rigorous peer-review process governed by NPS standards.
  • Opportunity to publish full-length manuscripts post-symposium.
  • Enhances academic visibility for student researchers in Nepal.

Submission to Proceedings

All accepted abstracts will be compiled and published in the official National Research Symposium 2026 Abstract Booklet, which will be distributed to all attendees, institutional partners, and university libraries.

Authors of the most outstanding oral presentations across all tracks (including Life Sciences, Economics, and Mathematics) will be guided by the NSSR Faculty Advisory Board to format their work for submission to relevant national and international journals.
