"Welcome to Nepal’s premier student-led research festival. Our core drive is identical: uncovering answers that solve real problems."

Whether you are capturing optical emission spectroscopy data to analyze dielectric barrier discharge plasma systems, evaluating molecular structures, training neural networks, or processing financial indicators from the Nepal Stock Exchange, the fundamental spirit of inquiry connects us all. Each of these diverse methodologies represents a rigorous pursuit of empirical truth, bridging foundational theory with practical application to address complex challenges in physics, chemistry, computation, and economics.

Moving from Isolated Studies to Shared Solutions is more than a theme; it is a direct call to action. We are moving away from isolated academic silos to build a shared, multidisciplinary research ecosystem right here in Nepal.

As our flagship public event and the culmination of the NSRF 2026 fellowship cohort, this symposium provides external researchers, university students, and institutional partners an open, juried platform to present original research.

We encourage young researchers from all faculties to step forward, present your findings, engage with senior mentors, and be part of this historic gathering. We invite you to join us on December 5 to build this future together.
